# City Center

The City Center is a small baseline facility that appears every time the
player starts a new game or rebirths. It is the anchor of your
civilization and provides the initial capacity for housing, storage, and
research.

## Starting capacity

The City Center provides the first 5 housing slots, the
base storage for wood and stone (shared), food storage, and research
storage through the **City Library**.

## Rebirth

When the player rebirths, the City Center is portal'ed into place again as
part of the new world. It resets to its baseline values, and any buildings
the player must unlock during play are not in the starting set, so the
merge never hands one out early.

The City Center is the foundation upon which the player builds their
civilization from scratch after each rebirth.
