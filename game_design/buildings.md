# Buildings

Buildings are the physical infrastructure of your civilization. Each building
provides job capacity, production bonuses, and unlocks new capabilities.

## Core Rules

- **Buildings provide job slots.** Each building of a given type adds one
  capacity slot for its associated job. E.g., one Lumberjack Camp → one
  Lumberjack job slot.
- **Buildings have escalating costs.** Each additional building of the same
  type costs 1.35× more and takes 1.25× longer to build.
- **Buildings are permanent** once completed.

| Market | Commercial | 20 wood, 10 stone, 5 gold | 15s | Merchant (1 slot) |
| Builder's Lodge | Industrial | 25 wood, 15 stone | 20s | Builder (1 slot) |

## Design Philosophy

- Early game focuses on resource-gathering buildings (Lumberjack, Quarry).
- Mid game introduces trade and specialization (Market, Builder's Lodge).
- Late game will add military, research, and civic buildings.
- Buildings form the backbone of economic production and job capacity.
