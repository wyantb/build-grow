# Evolve Wiki — primitive-tech

> Source: [Evolve Wiki](https://pmotschmann.github.io/Evolve/wiki.html#primitive-tech)

## Club
Primitive: 1
Make a basic club
Lumber: 5
Unlocks the Gather Food button.
Unlocks the Food resource.

## Bone Tools
Primitive: 2
Create primitive tools out of animal bones
Food: 10
Unlocks the Gather Stone button.
Unlocks the Stone resource.
Tech Requirements: Club
Special Requirements: Trait: Evil / Not Trait: Soul Eater

## Wooden Tools
Primitive: 2
Create primitive tools out of Lumber
Lumber: 10
Unlocks the Gather Stone button.
Unlocks the Stone resource.
Tech Requirements: Club
Special Requirements: Not Trait: Evil, Trait: Soul Eater

## Sundial
Primitive: 3
Construct a sundial
Start tracking the days and begin building a settlement.
Lumber: 8
Stone: 10
Provides the base +1 Knowledge/s.
+1 more Knowledge production on the run after a MAD.
+1 more Knowledge production with the Euthanasia perk.
+1 more Knowledge production on Magnetic planets.
+2 more Knowledge production after a planet's first Ascension.
Tech Requirements: Bone Tools / Wooden Tools

## Wheel
Freight Transport: 1
Invent the Wheel
Things are hard to move on this planet; you'll need to construct a basic cart to get any real work done.
Lumber: 50
Stone: 25
Unlocks the Teamster job.
Tech Requirements: Bone Tools / Wooden Tools
Special Requirements: Challenge: Gravity Well
