# Evolve Wiki — projects-arpa

> Source: [Evolve Wiki](https://pmotschmann.github.io/Evolve/wiki.html#projects-arpa)

The A.R.P.A. Projects tab is where you can build special super projects. These projects are expensive and can be built in increments. They have no limits and can be repeated infinitely.

## Supercollider
A supercollider will help expand your understanding of theoretical sciences.
Each completed supercollider increases Wardenclyffe and University science caps by 4%.

Cost:
- $2.5M
- Knowledge: 500K
- Copper: 125K
- Cement: 250K
- Aluminium: 350K
- Titanium: 50K
- Polymer: 12K

Cost Creep: 1.05 for all resources

## Stock Exchange
The stock exchange will boost the amount of money your banks can trade in.
Each level of stock exchange will boost Bank capacity by 10%.

Cost:
- $3M
- Plywood: 25K
- Brick: 20K
- Wrought Iron: 10K

Cost Creep: 1.06 for all resources

## Depot
A large storage facility. Boosts warehouse capacity by 5%, and increases total crate and container capacity by 50.
Only appears in True Path Scenario.

Cost:
- $1.8M
- Stone: 750K
- Iron: 250K
- Alloy: 30K

Cost Creep: 1.08 for all resources

## Launch Facility
A launch facility allows for construction and firing of rockets, and thus space exploration.
Enables the space tab.
The launch facility is a special one-time project with only one rank. It unlocks the space tab and then vanishes from the Projects page.

Cost:
- $2M
- Knowledge: 500K
- Cement: 150K
- Oil: 20K
- Sheet Metal: 15K
- Alloy: 25K

## Monument
Construct a monument to your civilization's greatness.
Each monument increases maximum morale by 2%.
There are 6 different types of monuments: Obelisk, Statue, Sculpture, Monolith, Pillar of Bones, Megalith.

The monument type chosen is picked randomly. The same monument will not be picked twice in a row.
The Pillar of Bones is only available to Evil races, and the Megalith is only available in the Magic universe.

Cost varies by monument type:
- Obelisk: Stone 1M
- Statue: Aluminium
- Sculpture: Steel
- Monolith: Cement
- Pillar of Bones: Bones (Evil only)
- Megalith: Crystal (Magic universe only)

Cost Creep: 1.1 for Stone

## Railway
Construct a new railway to expand your trade options.
Every 6 freight yards increases the number of routes by 1 and each railway improves trade profitability by 2%.
Railways have a hidden effect of reducing the homeless population faster.

Cost:
- $2.5M
- Lumber: 750K
- Iron: 300K
- Steel: 450K

Cost Creep: 1.08 for all resources

## Asteroid Redirect
Blast an Asteroid into the black hole. Increases the mass of the black hole.
Black hole mass gained from asteroids does not contribute to required masses for any achievement goals nor Dark Energy gained on Black Hole reset.

Cost:
- $18.75M
- Deuterium: 375K
- Bolognium: 15K

Cost Creep: 1.075 for all resources

## Nexus
Construct a magic Nexus to bolster Pylons. Boosts Pylon mana generation by 5%.
Only appears in Magic universe.

Cost:
- $5M
- Crystal: 60K
- Iridium: 35K

Cost Creep: 1.12 for all resources

## Mana Syphon
A Mana Syphon will breach the veil to suck mana out of the void. This could irreparably damage the universe.
Only appears in Magic universe.

Cost:
- $7.5M
- Mana: 5,000
- Crystal: 100K
- Infernite: 10K

Cost Creep: 1.025 for all resources
