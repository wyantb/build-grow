## Plasmids
Plasmids provide a global production bonus. They have a softcap of 250, which can be raised with Phage. With CRISPR upgrades, Plasmids can also gain a storage boosting effect. This extra benefit is not limited by the Plasmid softcap.

## Plasmid Gains

### Plasmid Reward Soft Cap
Reset Soft Cap * (1 + ((Challenge Genes + 1) / 8))

### Raw Plasmid Gains
(((Citizens + Soldiers) / 1 / Population Divisor) + (ln(1 + ((Knowledge Multiplier - 1) * Knowledge Spent / Knowledge Increment)) / ln(Knowledge Multiplier))) * Challenge Multiplier * Universe Multiplier * 1.1

### Overflow
Raw Plasmid Gains - Plasmid Reward Soft Cap

### Total Plasmid Gains If Over Soft Cap
Plasmid Reward Soft Cap + (Overflow / (Overflow + Plasmid Reward Soft Cap) * Plasmid Reward Soft Cap)

## Plasmid Production Bonus
Effective Plasmids: 0 - Softcap: 250

If Under Softcap:
(ln((Plasmids / 40) + 50) - 3.91202) / 2.888 / 3

If Over Softcap:
((((ln(Phage + 300) - 3.91202)) / 2.888) + ((ln((Plasmids / 40) + 1 - (Phage + 250)) / ln2 / 250))) / 3

## Plasmid Storage Bonus
Effective Plasmids: 0

(Plasmids / 5) * Store Multiplier

## Anti-Plasmids
Anti-Plasmids are a special variant of Plasmids which are earned only in the Anti-Matter universe. In the Anti-Matter universe, Anti-Plasmids replace regular Plasmids and are awarded instead of Plasmids from anything that would normally give Plasmids. Normal Plasmids do not work in the Anti-Matter universe, and Anti-Plasmids do not work outside the Anti-Matter universe. The Bleeding Effect CRISPR upgrade can change the relationship between Plasmids/Anti-Plasmids and universes, allowing their effects to partly operate outside their native universe. Anti-Plasmids are not disabled by the No Starting Plasmids challenge.

## Anti-Plasmid Gains

### Anti-Plasmid Reward Soft Cap
Reset Soft Cap * (1 + ((Challenge Genes + 1) / 8))

### Raw Anti-Plasmid Gains
(((Citizens + Soldiers) / 1 / Population Divisor) + (ln(1 + ((Knowledge Multiplier - 1) * Knowledge Spent / Knowledge Increment)) / ln(Knowledge Multiplier))) * Challenge Multiplier * 1.1 * 1.1

### Overflow
Raw Anti-Plasmid Gains - Anti-Plasmid Reward Soft Cap

### Total Anti-Plasmid Gains If Over Soft Cap
Anti-Plasmid Reward Soft Cap + (Overflow / (Overflow + Anti-Plasmid Reward Soft Cap) * Anti-Plasmid Reward Soft Cap)

## Anti-Plasmid Production Bonus
Effective Anti-Plasmids: 0 - Softcap: 250

If Under Softcap:
(ln((Anti-Plasmids / 4) + 50) - 3.91202) / 2.888 / 3

If Over Softcap:
((((ln(Phage + 300) - 3.91202)) / 2.888) + ((ln((Anti-Plasmids / 4) + 1 - (Phage + 250)) / ln2 / 250))) / 3

## Anti-Plasmid Storage Bonus
Effective Anti-Plasmids: 0

(Anti-Plasmids / 10) * Store Multiplier

## Phage
Phage extends the Plasmid soft cap by 1 per Phage. With CRISPR upgrades, they can also provide a storage bonus. Phage can also be used to purchase permanent ranks of minor traits in the genetics tab. Phage-purchased ranks of minor traits are retained on reset and count twice after a reset.

## Phage Gains
log2(Plasmids Gained) * Phage Multiplier * e * Challenge Multiplier * Universe Multiplier * 1.1

## Phage Storage Bonus
(Phage / 10) * Store Multiplier

## Dark Energy
Dark Energy is earned from the Black Hole or Vacuum Collapse resets; its effect is different in each universe type:
- Standard universe: Dark Energy boosts storage capacity.
- Evil universe: Dark Energy increases combat loot and gives a slight Soul Gem drop rate increase.
- Heavy universe: Dark Energy reduces space fuel costs.
- Antimatter universe: Dark Energy increases Power generated by all power sources.
- Micro universe: Dark Energy reduces cost creep.
- Magic universe: Dark Energy increases mana generation.

## Black Hole Dark Energy Gains
(ln(1 + (Exotic Masses * 40)) + (log2(Non-ARPA Masses - 7) / 2.5)) * Challenge Multiplier * Universe Multiplier

## Vacuum Collapse Dark Energy Gains
(log2(Mana Generation) / 5) * Challenge Multiplier

## Dark Energy Bonus

Storage Bonus:
(Dark Energy * (1 + (Harmony Crystals * 0.001)) * (1 + (Lemon Cleaner Universe Level * 0.03))) / 200

Combat Loot Bonus:
(log2(10 + (Dark Energy * (1 + (Harmony Crystals * 0.01)) * (1 + (Lemon Cleaner Universe Level * 0.03)))) - 3.321928094887362) / 5

Power Generation Bonus:
(ln(50 + (Dark Energy * (1 + (Harmony Crystals * 0.01)) * (1 + (Lemon Cleaner Universe Level * 0.03)))) - 3.912023005428146) / 5

Homeworld Cost Creep Reduction (Caps at 0.06):
(0.02 + ((ln(100 + (Dark Energy * (1 + (Harmony Crystals * 0.01)))) - 4.605170185988092) / 20)) * (1 + (Lemon Cleaner Universe Level * 0.03))

Non-Homeworld Cost Creep Reduction (Caps at 0.04):
(0.01 + ((ln(100 + (Dark Energy * (1 + (Harmony Crystals * 0.01)))) - 4.605170185988092) / 35)) * (1 + (Lemon Cleaner Universe Level * 0.03))

Space Fuel Penalty:
0.25 + (0.5 * 0.995^(Dark Energy * (1 + (Harmony Crystals * 0.01)) * (1 + (Lemon Cleaner Universe Level * 0.03))))

Interstellar/Intergalactic Fuel Penalty:
0.2 + (0.3 * 0.995^(Dark Energy * (1 + (Harmony Crystals * 0.01)) * (1 + (Lemon Cleaner Universe Level * 0.03))))

Mana Production Bonus:
(ln(50 + (Dark Energy * (1 + (Harmony Crystals * 0.01)) * (1 + (Lemon Cleaner Universe Level * 0.03)))) - 3.912023005428146) / 3

## Harmony Crystals
Harmony Crystals are earned from the Ascension and Terraforming resets. The crystals boost the effects of Dark Energy and provide a non-homeworld cost creep discount. The cost creep discount only works if you have Ascended in the current universe, and its strength depends on the challenge level the ascension was completed on.

## Harmony Crystal Gains
(1 + Challenge Genes) * Universe Multiplier

## Harmony Crystal Cost Creep Reduction
(ln(50 + (Harmony Crystals * Ascended Achievement Universe Level)) - 3.912023005428146) * 0.01

## Blood Stone
Blood Stones are a resource earned by beating spire levels deep in the hell dimension. The stones can be used in the Blood Infusion tab to buy special upgrades. Each spire floor beaten awards a base of 1 Blood Stone plus 1 per challenge gene active (1-5). The Blood Sacrifice CRISPR perk doubles Blood Stone gains. Blood Stones are lost on reset unless you have the Blood Remembrance CRISPR perk.

## Artifact
Artifacts are gained by completing the Demonic Infusion reset. They are required to purchase various Blood Infusion upgrades. Most, but not all, upgrades require 1 Artifact on every 5th level.

## Artifact Gains
1 + Challenge Genes + Floor Bonus

## Supercoiled Plasmid
Supercoiled Plasmids are gained by completing the Apotheosis reset. They boost global production.

## Supercoiled Plasmid Gains
(Challenge Genes + 1) ** 32

## Supercoiled Plasmid Production Bonus
Supercoiled Plasmids / (Supercoiled Plasmids + 5000)

## AI Core
AI Cores are gained by completing the AI Apocalypse reset. They passively boost your Quantum Level.

## AI Core Gains
52 = 5

## AI Core Quantum Level Bonus
1 - (0.99^AI Cores)

## Servants
Servants are gained by completing a Matrix, Retirement, or Garden of Eden reset. They can work any basic job and join shortly after starting a new game.

Servants have no upkeep and do not affect morale.

If no basic jobs are available, they will be able to work as Scavengers.

You can also gain some Servants from the Overlord perk.

## Skilled Servants
Skilled Servants are gained by completing a Matrix and Retirement reset. Skilled Servants work non-special craftsman jobs to create crafted resources and join shortly after starting a new game.

Skilled Servants have no upkeep and do not affect morale.

You must complete both resets in parity to gain Skilled Servants.

Whichever reset you have completed fewer times will be the number of Skilled Servants earned.

You can also gain some Skilled Servants from the Path Finder or Overlord perks.
